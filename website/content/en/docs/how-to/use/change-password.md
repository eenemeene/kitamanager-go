You want to change your own password. Self-service.

## Steps

1. Open the user menu at the top right of any page → **Settings**.
2. In the **Password** card, enter your **current password** and the **new password** twice.
3. Click **Change password**.

Every other browser signed in to your account is signed out automatically. Your current session stays signed in.

## Notes

- The new password must meet the application's minimum length. Pick a long, distinct password from a manager — there's no recovery if you also lose your authenticator and recovery codes.
- If an admin reset your password (because you locked yourself out), you'll be asked to change it on next sign-in for the same reason.
- For *another* user's password (admin only), see [Reset a user's password](../../administer/reset-user-password/).
