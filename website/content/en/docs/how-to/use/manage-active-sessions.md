---
title: Manage active sessions
weight: 13
---

You want to see every browser currently signed in to your account, and revoke any you don't recognise.

## Steps

1. Open the user menu → **Settings**.
2. The **Active sessions** card lists every active session: device label, IP, last-active time. Your current session is marked.
3. Click **Revoke** next to any session you don't recognise. That browser is signed out immediately.

## Notes

- Changing your password automatically revokes every other session.
- If you don't recognise an active session, treat it as a compromise: revoke it, then change your password and review the audit log.
- Sessions never expire silently from inactivity in this version — they remain valid until you sign out, the cookie expires, or you revoke them. There's no "remember me for 30 days" toggle.
