title: Move children between sections
weight: 3
---

You want to move a child from one section to another (e.g. they've outgrown the Nest group).

## Quick way: drag and drop

1. Click **Sections** in the sidebar.
2. Each section is a column. Find the child's card and drag it to the target section column.
3. Drop. KitaManager creates a new contract in the target section starting today and ends the old contract yesterday.

## Manual way: edit the contract

When you need to set a specific change date (not "today"):

1. Open the child's detail page.
2. End the current contract by setting its **To** date.
3. Click **Create Contract** and set **From** to the day after, with the new section.

## Notes

- Drag and drop is the right tool for "this child moves up to the next group". The "manual" path is for advance planning ("Max moves to Große on August 1").
- The dashboard's **Children Over Section Age Limit** widget surfaces children who've passed their section's max age — moving them is the fix.
- For the section-age model, see [What the staffing key means](../../../explanation/what-the-staffing-key-means/).
