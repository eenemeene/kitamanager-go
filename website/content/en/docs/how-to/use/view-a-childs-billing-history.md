title: View a child's billing history
weight: 16
---

You want to see every ISBJ-billed amount for one child across all uploaded Bescheide.

## Steps

1. Click **Children** in the sidebar.
2. Open the child's detail page.
3. Click **Billing history** (or scroll to the Billing section).

The page shows one row per month, with the calculated amount, the billed amount, the delta, and the status. Mismatches are highlighted.

## Notes

- Only months that exist as uploaded Bescheide appear. If a child started in January but the earliest bill on file is from March, January and February rows are absent — they're not zero-filled.
- The child has to have a voucher number assigned for any of these rows to match. If you see lots of "missing from bill", check [Assign a Kita-Gutschein number](../assign-a-voucher/) first.
- For drilling into one specific bill instead, open it from the **Funding Bills** page.
