title: Use the forecast to plan intake or hires
weight: 8
---

You want to project the next Kita year and see what changing intake or staffing would do to your finances.

The Forecast page has three tabs that answer three different questions.

## Optimize — how many children to reach a target balance?

Use this when the question is "how full do I need to be?"

1. Click **Statistics** → **Forecast** → **Optimize**.
2. Set a **target cumulative balance** (the surplus or deficit you want to land on at the end of the Kita year).
3. Pick the sections you'd grow into and a **max children per section per month**.
4. Click **Find Optimal Children Count**.

KitaManager simulates the year with growing intake counts until it finds the smallest count that reaches the target. The result lands in the **Children** tab so you can review and adjust.

If the target isn't reachable within your limits, widen the section or per-month limit, or lower the target.

## Children — model intake and departures

Use this for "what if we take three more 1-year-olds in November?"

1. Click **Forecast** → **Children**.
2. **Add Child** with count, age, dates, section, and contract properties.
3. To model a departure, click an existing child in the **Remove Child** list. Click again to undo.

## Employees — model hires and departures

Same shape as Children, for staff. Useful for "what if we hire one S 8a step 3 at 30h starting October?"

## Calculate and read

Click **Calculate Forecast**. The results panel shows the projected year as tabbed charts: cumulative balance, financials, children, staffing, occupancy, employee hours.

- **Show Baseline** overlays the unmodified projection so you can see your scenario's effect.
- **Reset** clears every change.

## Notes

- The forecast assumes contracts run unchanged for the whole year and that funding rates don't change. Treat it as a *direction*, not a contract.
- Numbers carry through to euros: the income side uses your current funding rates, the cost side uses the active pay plan and includes the employer contribution.
- The Optimize tab is purely additive — it doesn't *remove* children to reach a smaller balance. Use Children → Remove for that.
