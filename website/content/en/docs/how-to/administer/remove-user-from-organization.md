---
title: Remove a user from an organization
weight: 6
---

You want to revoke a user's access to your organisation without deleting the account.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **Users** → click the user.
2. In the **Organization memberships** section, find the organisation.
3. Click **Remove**.

The user retains their account and any membership in *other* organisations. They can no longer see or change anything in this organisation.

## Notes

- If the user has access to no other organisations after removal, they can still sign in but won't see any data — consider [deactivating](../edit-or-deactivate-a-user/) the account instead.
- The audit log records the removal in your organisation's log.
