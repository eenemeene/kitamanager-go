---
title: Update organization name and state
weight: 1
---

You want to change your organisation's display name or its German federal state.

## Steps

1. Sign in as an admin (or superadmin).
2. Open **Settings** → **Organization** in the sidebar.
3. Update **Name** or **State (Bundesland)** as needed.
4. Click **Save**.

## Notes

- The state determines which government funding rules apply. Today only **berlin** ships with funding rates loaded; other states will mismatch on every bill until rates for that state are imported.
- Only **superadmins** can create or delete an organisation. Admins can rename and update state.
- The change is recorded in the audit log.
